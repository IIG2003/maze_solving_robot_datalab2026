Path start-relative odometry, metres

How to read this

Speed, turn rate and clearance come from the robot's own sensors; wheel speeds are what the controller commanded. Acceleration is the change of measured speed (smoothed over 0.1 s) and the change of commanded speed (the controller's slew limit shows up as flat steps).

Hover any chart (or tab to it and press the arrow keys) to read the values; every chart and the path share one cursor. Use the phase buttons to look at one part of the course.

Tilt near 37° means the robot has tipped onto its nose. A closest-beam reading under 3 cm means the body is touching something.
